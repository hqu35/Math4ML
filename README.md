# Math for Machine Learning

### A Math-First Approach to Understanding Machine Learning

*A personal learning journey and an experimental attempt to make the mathematics behind modern machine learning more intuitive, motivated, and rigorous.*

## Overview

This repository documents my ongoing exploration of the mathematical foundations of machine learning, drawing primarily from undergraduate and graduate-level courses at the **University of Wisconsin–Madison**, **Stanford University**, and other publicly available academic resources.

As an undergraduate student with a strong interest in mathematics and machine learning, I have often found myself facing a particular challenge: many introductory ML resources emphasize computational intuition while leaving important mathematical details unexplained, whereas more theoretical textbooks can introduce sophisticated abstractions without sufficiently motivating why they are necessary.

**My goal is to explore a middle ground: mathematical rigor without sacrificing intuition.**

Rather than simply collecting definitions, theorems, and proofs, I want to understand the reasoning that motivates them:

- What problem is a particular mathematical concept trying to solve?
- Why is a definition formulated in precisely this way?
- How might we discover a proof rather than merely verify it?
- How do seemingly abstract mathematical structures connect to modern machine learning?

## The Role of AI-Assisted Learning

One of the motivations behind this project is the rapidly changing role of artificial intelligence in education.

I believe AI-assisted learning offers an interesting opportunity: mathematical ideas that once required considerable time and effort to interpret can now be explored interactively through carefully structured questions, alternative explanations, counterexamples, and proof discussions.

In particular, I have found **ChatGPT especially useful for exploring the motivation behind mathematical definitions and discovering the intuition behind rigorous proofs.**

However, AI is not a substitute for mathematical reasoning.

All formal mathematical claims, derivations, and proofs included in this repository are intended to be independently examined, verified, and refined against reliable academic references.

The goal is not to let AI do the mathematics, but to investigate how AI can help students **understand mathematics more deeply and efficiently**.

## Long-Term Vision: Experimental Lecture Notes

Beyond documenting my learning progress, I hope to gradually develop this repository into a coherent set of lecture notes tentatively titled:

**Mathematics for Machine Learning: A Motivated and Rigorous Introduction**

The intended audience is undergraduate students with some mathematical maturity who want to transition efficiently into the mathematical foundations of modern machine learning.

Unlike a traditional textbook organized primarily around theorems and their proofs, these notes will emphasize a recurring structure:

**Motivation → Intuition → Formal Definition → Rigorous Derivation → Applications**

Where appropriate, I also plan to include selected exercises, counterexamples, and connections to machine learning algorithms.

My tentative goal is to assemble an initial, reasonably complete version before finishing my undergraduate studies.

This is an exploratory educational project, not a finished textbook or an official course resource.
